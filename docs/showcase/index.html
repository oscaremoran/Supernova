Supernova · what's new

Animated recreations of the latest additions, drawn with the mod's real sprites. Behaviour and numbers match the game; effects are close approximations.

Acid death bursts BugsPredators

Every Alien bursts into acid when it dies: violet for Bugs, crimson for Predators. Click an Alien to pop it.

Bugs swarm exposed buildings Hatchling · Malign

Bugs march to your core, but break off to eat any building within ~14 tiles that they have a clear line to. The Bolter guards the path; the lone drill up top does not.

Hives spread Hive

Left alone, a Hive buds a new one 9–15 tiles away every 4 minutes. It stops at 3 per cluster (12 per map), and never buds within 12 tiles of your buildings. Time-lapse: 4 game minutes ≈ 3 seconds.

Predators hunt the wounded Leacher · Ripper

Predators always shoot the most damaged unit in range. Watch the red line: it jumps to whichever Sentry has the lowest health.

Gunships break an Alien base Sentry · Warden · Bastion

Built in the Foundry. In the simulator, 2 Bastions destroy an Acid Spire and two Acid Pods without losses.

Igniter and Arc Coil vs a swarm new turrets

The Igniter (left) burns Volatiles: its flames pierce and set Bugs on fire, and one beat 14 Hatchlings in testing. The Arc Coil (right) throws chain lightning on power alone.
